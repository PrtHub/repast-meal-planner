The carb cap

Every day stops short of the wall.

Most trackers add up your carbs after you have eaten them, then turn the number red. Repast works the other way round. Each day is built under your cap before you ever see it, so there is nothing to undo.

This is one real week from the planner, at a 20g net cap and 1,690 calories a day.

Inside the app

Less deciding. More eating.

  1. Today

    Open it and the day is already decided.

    Net carbs left today, protein and fat against your targets, and the next meal with its photo. Tick it off when you have eaten it.

  2. Week

    Seven days, each under your cap.

    Every day shows its carbs against your limit. Swap a meal, lock the ones you love, or rebuild the days ahead. Leftovers are planned in.

  3. Recipes

    Every recipe scaled to your portion.

    Ingredient amounts follow your plan, so the macros match what you eat. Out of something? Each swap shows how it changes the carbs.

  4. Cook mode

    One step at a time, timers included.

    Large type you can read from the hob. Tap a time in a step to start a timer, and it keeps running while you move on.

  5. Shop

    One list for the whole week.

    Sorted by aisle and rounded to the packs you will actually buy. Mark the staples you already have and they stay off the list.

  6. Ate out

    Ate something else? The day adjusts.

    Log a rough guess at the carbs and the size. The meals left today are resized to fit what is left of your cap.

  7. Assistant

    Questions? Ask about the dish.

    Swaps, timing, or how it fits your carbs today, answered from the recipe in front of you. Part of Repast Pro, with a daily limit.

The details

The small things that keep a week going.

  • Make-ahead, flagged the night before

    Dishes that need starting the evening before show up that evening, so breakfast is never a surprise.

  • Leftovers planned in

    Cook once, eat twice. The week counts whole cooks, and so does the shopping list.

  • Swaps with the carb difference

    Out of an ingredient? Each substitute shows what it does to the carbs before you use it.

  • Electrolytes, early on

    Sodium, potassium and magnesium for the day, through your first weeks of keto.

  • A weight trend, not daily noise

    Log the scale and watch the smoothed line. After a couple of weeks Repast can estimate your real maintenance calories and offer to use them.

  • Weeks in a row, not a streak

    Your week shows what you logged. Missing a day does not wipe your progress.

  • Reminders that name the dish

    An evening nudge with tonight’s dinner in it. Off until you turn it on.

  • Your cooking time

    Choose under 15, 25, 40 or 60 minutes, or any length, and the plan stays inside it.

Questions

Before you start.

Is it only for keto?

Keto comes first, with a 20g net carb cap by default. You can also plan low-carb, high-protein, paleo or Mediterranean weeks.

Do I have to log everything I eat?

No. You tick off the meals from your plan. If you eat something else, log a rough guess and the rest of the day adjusts around it.

Can it work around allergies or a vegetarian diet?

Yes. Choose vegetarian, pescatarian or no red meat, and leave out dairy, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, gluten, soy, fish, shellfish, pork or beef. Always check labels yourself if an allergy is serious.

Net carbs or total carbs?

Either. Pick one in setup and every number in the app follows it.

How long do the recipes take?

You set the limit: under 15, 25, 40 or 60 minutes, or any length. The plan only uses recipes that fit.

Is my data private?

There is no account. Your measurements, plans, meal log and weight stay on your phone. The privacy policy lists exactly what leaves it and why.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Repast is on iPhone for now.

How do I cancel?

In your App Store account settings, at any time. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends and you are not charged again.

Is this medical advice?

No. Repast is a planning tool. Talk to your doctor before starting a keto or low-carb diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication.

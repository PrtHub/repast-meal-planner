## 4. Allergies and food safety

free of an allergen.** Products vary by brand and country, labels change, and
kitchens cross-contaminate. Always read the labels of what you buy, and if you
have a serious allergy, check every ingredient yourself.

Cook food to safe temperatures. Poultry and minced meat should reach 74°C (165°F)
in the middle. Follow the doneness cues in each recipe, and use your own judgement
about food that looks, smells or tastes wrong.
